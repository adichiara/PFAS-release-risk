Residents by PFAS6 in their tap water

Since 2020 every community system has had to test its treated water for PFAS6 (the sum of PFOA, PFOS, PFHxS, PFNA, PFHpA and PFDA) against the 20 ng/L state standard. Now is a system's mean over its last 12 months of results; small systems that moved to reduced monitoring after low early results may have their latest results from 2021 or 2022. Highest year is its highest calendar-year mean, usually before treatment was installed. Non-detects count as zero.

Method

  • Results: finished-water PFAS6 per system from MassDEP's drinking-water data (EEA Data Portal), queried per system and water type. The API returns at most 100 results per query, so about 40 heavily sampled systems are represented by a sample of their results.
  • Purchased water: systems that buy all their water take their seller's results, following EPA SDWIS buyer-seller links up to three hops. The __MWRA_SYSTEMS__ systems on MWRA reservoir water (__MWRA_POP__ residents) report PFAS6 not detected.
  • Residents: 2020 census blocks take the community system whose MassDEP service area contains them; populated blocks outside every service area are the private-well population used by the study.
  • Not covered: non-community systems (schools, workplaces), bottled water, and exposure at levels below reporting limits.
