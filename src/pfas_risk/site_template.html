What drives the scores

How much the model's held-out ROC AUC drops when one group of features is shuffled (count inside, count within 2 km and distance to the nearest site together). Larger drops mean the model leans on that group more. Correlated groups share credit: industrial sites cluster together, so shuffling one sector leaves others carrying similar information. This shows association with reported releases, not cause.

__IMPORTANCE_CHART__

Drinking-water PFAS uses only samples collected before __FWD_CUTOFF_YEAR__. The forward test, which scores only releases reported after the cutoff, is the check that it still helps.

How well it validates

Five-fold cross-validation that holds out whole towns, repeated __REPEATS__ times. "Smoothed" models blend each cell's score with its neighbors' within 3 km, computed inside each fold so no score depends on a model that saw that cell. The highlighted row is the model shown on the map.

Method

  • Unit: equal-area __UNITS__ clipped to the state (Massachusetts State Plane meters). Population, housing and land area come from 2020 census blocks, weighted by the share of each block in a cell; each cell belongs to the town holding most of its land.
  • Response: MassDEP reportable releases (RTNs) with PFAS as a chemical, located by matching addresses to MassGIS address points.
  • Features: counts inside, counts within 2 km and distance to the nearest fire station, MassDEP major facility, hazardous-waste generator, air-permitted facility and underground storage tank, and to EPA-registered facilities in eight PFAS-related industries (textiles, paper, chemicals and plastics, metal finishing, electronics, petroleum, aviation and military, waste and wastewater); public water supply wells and intakes tested for PFAS6, with PFAS6 detected, and at or above the 20 ng/L standard, using only samples collected before __FWD_CUTOFF_YEAR__ so the forward test stays honest; landfills; aquifers; major-road density; population and housing density. Airports, military installations, EPA Toxics Release Inventory reporters and sewer service areas were tested and left out because they did not improve the model (see Data sources).
  • Validation: five-fold cross-validation holding out whole towns, a size- and density-matched permutation test, and a forward-in-time test on releases reported after a cutoff.
  • Map scores are out-of-fold: each cell is colored by a model trained without its town, so known release sites are not simply echoed back.
