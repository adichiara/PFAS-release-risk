About this map

Areas outside community water service, where homes use private wells, show the estimated chance that groundwater there is at or above the 20 ng/L PFAS6 standard: a model trained on public-well results, calibrated to MassDEP's testing of 1,649 private wells. Public water systems can be added, colored by measured PFAS6 in treated water now or in their worst year.

Private-well estimates are for areas, not individual wells. Only a test tells a household what is in its water.
