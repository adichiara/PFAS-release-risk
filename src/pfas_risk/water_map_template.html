PFAS in Massachusetts drinking water

Public water systems are colored by their measured PFAS6 in treated water. Areas outside public water service, where homes use private wells, show the estimated chance that groundwater there has PFAS6 at or above the 20 ng/L state standard, calibrated to MassDEP's testing of 1,649 private wells.

Private-well estimates are rough (see the method page); only a test of the well itself tells a household what is in its water.
